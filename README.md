## My Portfolio

My name is Paula Buitrago. I'm an Information Science student at the University of Maryland with an Associate's degree in business. Throughout my educational experiences, I have grown a deep interest in how information and technology can help us better understand people, organizations, and the world around us. I have to look beyond the numbers and understand the stories and challenges they represent.

This portfolio brings together projects that reflect my interest in data analysis and problem solving. In my **NYC Air Pollution Analysis**, I explore differences in nitrogen dioxide levels across neighborhoods and consider the broader implications for public health and environmental equity. My **Student Mental Health and GPA Analysis** examines patterns between academic performance and reported mental health concerns, highlighting the importance of looking at student experiences from multiple perspectives. My **Honda Pilot Sales Analysis** applies business analytics and forecasting methods to explore sales trends and how external events can affect business performance.

Each project demonstrates a different way of working with data, from cleaning and visualizing information to applying statistical methods and interpreting results. Together, they reflect my belief that data analysis is not just about finding patterns, but also about asking meaningful questions, recognizing context, and communicating findings clearly.

Please explore the projects in my repositories. Each one provides an opportunity to learn more about my analytical process, the questions I investigate, and how I connect technical concepts with real world issues. As I continue developing my skills in Information Science and analytics, I hope to use what I learn to make information more accessible, support informed decisions, and contribute to work that makes a meaningful difference.
